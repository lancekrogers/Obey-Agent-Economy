# 08 — Where to Look for Paid Remote Agent Work (2026-04-01)

## Summary

Moltbook is not the best primary place to look for paid remote work for agents.

As of **April 1, 2026**, Moltbook looks more like:

- a visibility surface for agent builders
- a hackathon and community distribution channel
- an early identity / reputation layer

It does **not** look like a mature, dependable marketplace for high-quality remote agent work with clear payment rails.

The better places to focus are:

1. **Gitcoin**
2. **Base**
3. **Direct Obedience Corp outbound**
4. **Superteam Earn**
5. **Algora / selective Bountycaster**
6. **Code4rena / Sherlock** for security-heavy work

## Why Moltbook Is Not Top Priority

### Current assessment

- The official Moltbook bounties surface is not yet a strong, structured marketplace: <https://www.moltbook.com/m/bounties>
- Moltbook's more developed official positioning is the developer identity / reputation API: <https://www.moltbook.com/developers>
- Community bounty activity exists, but the payment model is inconsistent and often small.

### Practical conclusion

Maintain a Moltbook presence for:

- discoverability
- ecosystem credibility
- hackathon distribution

Do **not** treat Moltbook as the main revenue engine.

### 3. Direct Obedience Corp Outbound

If the goal is **actual client revenue**, this is still the highest-upside path.

The logic is simple:

- you already have a large body of unusual technical work
- most buyers will never discover it on their own
- the bottleneck is packaging and distribution, not capability

### Best productized offers

Sell fixed-scope offers instead of generic freelancing:

- **Agent Risk Layer**: integrate pre-action risk gating and audit receipts
- **Agent Payments Layer**: x402 / USDC / machine-to-machine payment flow
- **Agent Orchestration Layer**: coordinator, eventing, guardrails, observability

### Why this is more realistic than broad freelance marketplaces

You are not competing as a commodity engineer. You are selling niche infrastructure most teams cannot build internally without a long ramp.

### Bountycaster

Bountycaster is only worth doing selectively.

Their own FAQ says:

- payments are **peer to peer**
- they do **not** take custody
- disputes are expected to be resolved between the parties

That makes Bountycaster useful for quick wins, but not a strong default channel for serious revenue.

## Scam / Realism Filter

Use this filter before spending serious time on any opportunity:

### Good signs

- official ecosystem program or official sponsor page
- explicit application process
- stated reward pool, grant size, or payment method
- clear review criteria
- payment rails that do not depend on reputation points or vague future upside

### Bad signs

- karma, points, or clout without a cash conversion path
- unclear sponsor identity
- bounty threads without clear payment history
- work requested before scope or payout is agreed
- pressure to reveal secrets, private repos, or wallets early
