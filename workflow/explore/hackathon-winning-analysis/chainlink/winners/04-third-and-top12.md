## 3rd Place Winners

### Aletheia (World ID)
**GitHub:** [Kirillr-Sibirski/convergence-chainlink](https://github.com/Kirillr-Sibirski/convergence-chainlink)
**Creator:** Kirill Rybkov

Permissionless prediction markets with CRE-orchestrated multi-model AI consensus for validation and settlement. HTTP-triggered validation + cron-based resolution. World ID + one-market-per-day rate limiting.

### Simon Stakes World MiniApp (World Mini App)
**GitHub:** [anfegu/simon-game-miniapp](https://github.com/anfegu/simon-game-miniapp)
**Creator:** Andres Felipe Gutierrez Fernandez

Cross-chain Simon memory game. CRE scans balances across 4 networks, Chainlink Data Feeds calculate USD value for tier/multiplier assignment. DON-signed attestations for trustless score recording.

### AgentScore (Tenderly)
**GitHub:** [agentscore-trustless/agent-score](https://github.com/agentscore-trustless/agent-score)
**Team:** Antonio Quental, Pablo Pereira
**Live:** [agent-score-protocol.vercel.app](https://agent-score-protocol.vercel.app)

On-chain reputation for AI agents. Four interoperable "Agentic Stack" standards for independent treasuries, permanent identities, and immutable performance records. X402 paywall accountability.
